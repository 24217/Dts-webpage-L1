Our Oceans Need Us

Our oceans cover most of our planet and are home to millions of plants and animals. However, they are facing a growing threat from pollution. Plastic waste, chemicals, oil, and other rubbish are entering the ocean every day, harming marine life and damaging ecosystems. Ocean pollution can affect everything from tiny organisms to large animals, and it can even impact humans through the food we eat and the water we depend on. By understanding the causes and effects of ocean pollution, we can learn how to protect our oceans and keep them healthy for future generations.
