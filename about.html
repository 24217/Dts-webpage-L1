About Ocean Pollution

About Our Website

Our website was created to raise awareness about ocean pollution and explain how it affects our oceans and marine life. Pollution from plastic, rubbish and harmful chemicals can damage marine ecosystems and put animals at risk. We want to help people understand why keeping our oceans clean is important and encourage everyone to make small changes to protect the environment.

Why Is Ocean Pollution a Problem?

Ocean pollution is a serious environmental issue. Plastic bags, bottles, fishing nets and other rubbish can end up in the sea. Marine animals may mistake plastic for food or become trapped in rubbish. Chemicals and oil can also contaminate the water and harm marine habitats. Pollution can affect the food chain and damage the ecosystems that many animals depend on.

How Can We Help?

Everyone can help reduce ocean pollution through small everyday actions. Using reusable bags and drink bottles, recycling correctly, avoiding unnecessary single-use plastics and putting rubbish in bins can all help reduce waste. Taking part in beach clean-ups is another way to stop rubbish from entering the ocean.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage people to care for the ocean and understand the impact their actions can have on the environment. By learning more about ocean pollution and making responsible choices, we can all contribute to a cleaner and healthier ocean for future generations.
